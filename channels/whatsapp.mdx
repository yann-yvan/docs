Shortext uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). You connect your numbers by logging in to Meta from Shortext: no token to copy, no webhook to configure.

## Before you start

- A Facebook account with access to your company's **Meta Business** portfolio (or the right to create one).
- A phone number that can receive an SMS or a call to verify it, and that is **not** registered in the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app (or that you are ready to migrate).
- A payment method in Meta Business for the conversations Meta bills.

## The 24-hour window

WhatsApp protects users from unsolicited messages:

- When a contact writes to you, a **24-hour window** opens. During that window, your agent and your team can send any message: text, media, interactive.
- Outside the window, a business can only send an **approved template**. This is also how you write first to a contact.

In practice:

| Situation | What you can send |
| --- | --- |
| The contact wrote less than 24 hours ago | Anything |
| The contact never wrote, or wrote more than 24 hours ago | An approved template only |

When your team replies from the Inbox outside the window, Shortext sends the reply as a notification template so it still reaches the contact. Through the API, starting a conversation with free text outside the window is refused: use a template. See [Start a conversation](/api-reference/guides/start-a-conversation).
