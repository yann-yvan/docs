A template is a message model approved by Meta. It is the only kind of message a business can send to a contact outside the [24-hour window](/channels/whatsapp#the-24-hour-window), for example to send an order update or to start a conversation.

## Anatomy of a template

- **Name** and **language**: a template exists in one or more languages (`fr`, `en_US`...). You send it by name and language.
- **Category**: `UTILITY` (transactional updates), `MARKETING` (promotions) or `AUTHENTICATION` (one-time codes). Meta prices conversations by category.
- **Header** (optional): a text, or an image, a video or a document.
- **Body**: the text, with **variables** such as `{{name}}` and `{{order}}`.
- **Footer** (optional): a short text.
- **Buttons** (optional): quick replies, a URL, a phone number, a code to copy.

## Manage templates in the dashboard

Open a WhatsApp number in **Communication > Channels > WhatsApp Business** and go to its templates.

- **Create** a template: fill in the parts above and add variables to the body.
- **Publish** it to submit it to Meta. It is `PENDING` until Meta approves (`APPROVED`) or rejects it (`REJECTED`, with a reason).
- **Sync** pulls the templates created directly in Meta Business Manager, and refreshes their status.

## Send a template

- From the dashboard: in a **campaign** to a segment of contacts, or from the Inbox when the window is closed.
- From the API: `POST /conversations` with `message.type` `template`. See [Send a template](/api-reference/guides/send-a-template).
