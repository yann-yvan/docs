These features are in progress. They are not available yet; their names and behavior may change before release.

| Feature | What it will do |
| --- | --- |
| Gmail and Outlook | Connect a Google or Microsoft mailbox in one click (OAuth), without IMAP / SMTP settings or app passwords. |
| Hosted template builder | A Shortext page, opened from your product, where your clients create WhatsApp templates and submit them to Meta. |
| Instagram and Messenger | Answer Instagram direct messages and Facebook Messenger conversations with your agents. |

Until then:

- connect Gmail and Outlook mailboxes with [IMAP / SMTP](/channels/mailbox);
- create templates in the dashboard, see [WhatsApp templates](/channels/whatsapp-templates).

Want one of these features early? Write to [hello@ny-corp.io](mailto:hello@ny-corp.io).
