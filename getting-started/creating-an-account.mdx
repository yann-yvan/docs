---
title: Create your account
description: Sign up, verify your email and create your company to reach the Shortext dashboard.
---

It takes a few minutes to get from sign-up to your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [shortext.ny-corp.io/dash](https://shortext.ny-corp.io/dash) and choose to create an account. Enter your name, your email address and a password.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/signup-page.png" alt="Shortext sign-up page" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>Use your work email: it is the address Shortext uses for account and billing notifications.</Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Shortext sends you a verification email. Click the link in it to activate your account.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/email-verification.png" alt="Email verification message" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your company">
    Everything in Shortext belongs to a company. The company wizard asks for:

    - general information: the company name, its country, its industry and its size;
    - contact information, such as the company email;
    - additional information, such as a logo.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/profile-setup.png" alt="Company creation wizard" />
    </Frame>

    You can create or join other companies later and switch between them from the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach your dashboard">
    You land on the dashboard of your company. Continue with the [platform overview](/getting-started/platform-overview).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/getting-started/dashboard-overview.png" alt="Shortext dashboard" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite your team

Members are invited from the company settings, each with a role (admin, manager, agent, developer...). The role decides which parts of the dashboard a member can use, for example only the Inbox for an agent.
