The dashboard at [shortext.ny-corp.io/dash](https://shortext.ny-corp.io/dash) is organized in sections in the left sidebar. What you see depends on your role in the company and on your plan.

## Inbox

Where your team follows conversations live and answers customers.

- **All**: every conversation of the company.
- **Mine**: conversations assigned to you.
- **Unassigned**: conversations nobody has picked up yet.

See [Inbox](/inbox/overview).

## Conversations

The history of all conversations and the **tickets** opened from them. See [Tickets](/inbox/tickets).

## Customers

Your **contacts** (everyone who wrote to you or whom you wrote to) and **segments** to group them, for example for campaigns.

## Marketing

**Campaigns** send a WhatsApp template to a segment of contacts.

## Communication

The configuration of your automation:

- **AI Agents**: create agents, write their instructions, give them tools and knowledge. See [Agents](/agents/creating-an-agent).
- **Channels**: connect WhatsApp numbers, mailboxes and Shortext endpoints, and choose the agent answering on each. See [Channels](/channels/overview).
- **Knowledge bases**: documents your agents can search to answer.

## Billing

Your plan, your credit balance and usage, and your invoices.

## Developer

Everything for integrators:

- **API Keys**: create the keys your systems use to call the API. See [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication).
- **Webhooks**: endpoints where Shortext pushes events. See [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks).

## Company settings

The company profile, members and roles, and notification preferences are in the company menu and in your user profile.
