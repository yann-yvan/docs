The Inbox gathers the conversations of all your channels (WhatsApp, mailboxes, Shortext endpoints) in one place. Your agents answer automatically; your team watches, steps in and follows up.

## Views

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every conversation of the company. |
| **Mine** | The conversations assigned to you. |
| **Unassigned** | The conversations nobody has picked up. |

Members with the **agent** role only see what their role allows: typically the conversations, not the configuration.

## A conversation

Opening a conversation shows:

- the messages, whatever their author: the contact, the AI agent, a member of your team, or your system through the API;
- the contact (name, identifier, language) and the channel;
- the current session: open (active) or closed (archived), whether a human took it over, and who it is assigned to.

## Answer a customer

Type your message and send it. You can also send media, documents and interactive messages from the message builder.

<Note>
Replying takes the conversation over: the AI agent stops answering this contact until you hand it back. See [Handover and assignment](/inbox/handover-and-assignment).
</Note>

On WhatsApp, if the contact has not written in the last 24 hours, Shortext sends your reply as a notification template so it can reach them. See [the 24-hour window](/channels/whatsapp#the-24-hour-window).

## From the API

Everything the Inbox does is also available to your systems with the API v2.0: list conversations and their messages, reply, take over, hand back and assign. See the [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction).
