title: What is Shortext?
description: Shortext runs AI agents that answer your customers on WhatsApp, by email and inside your own apps, with your team ready to take over.
---

Shortext is a platform for businesses that talk to their customers on messaging channels. You create **agents**, connect them to **channels** (a WhatsApp Business number, a mailbox, your own app), and the agents answer customers around the clock. When a conversation needs a person, someone from your team takes it over from the **Inbox**.

- **API Reference** is for developers who integrate Shortext with their own systems. Start with the [API quickstart](/getting-started/api-quickstart).

## Typical uses

| Use | How Shortext helps |
| --- | --- |
| Customer support | The agent answers common questions, a human takes over the rest from the Inbox. |
| Order and delivery updates | Your system sends WhatsApp templates through the API, the agent handles the replies. |
| Sales follow-up | Start conversations with approved templates and let the agent qualify the leads. |
| In-app assistant | Your app sends user messages to a Shortext endpoint and shows the agent's replies. |
