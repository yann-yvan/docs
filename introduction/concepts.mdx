## Company

Everything in Shortext belongs to a **company**: agents, channels, conversations, API keys and webhooks. Your user account can be a member of several companies; each member has a role (for example admin, manager or agent) that decides what they can see and do in the dashboard.

## Agent

An **agent** answers your customers. It has:

- a **name** and an **active** switch: an inactive agent keeps receiving and storing messages, but does not answer;
- **instructions**: the system prompt the AI follows (tone, rules, what to do and not to do);
- an **AI** switch: when the AI is off, the agent sends a fixed **inactive message** instead;
- **tools**: your own HTTP APIs the agent can call to look up or change data, see [Tools](/agents/tools-configuration);
- a **knowledge base** it can search to answer.

Your plan sets how many agents your company can have.

## Channel

A **channel** is where conversations happen. Shortext supports three types:

| Type | What it is | Channel id |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `whatsapp` | A WhatsApp Business number connected through Meta | The WhatsApp phone number id |
| `mail` | A mailbox connected through IMAP / SMTP | `mbx_...` |
| `shortext` | An endpoint your own server or app talks to through the API | `ste_...` |

Each channel is attached to **one agent**, which answers on it. A channel can also be **detached**: messages are still stored, nobody answers automatically.

## Contact

A **contact** is a customer. It is identified by its **identifier** on the channel: a phone number on WhatsApp, an email address on a mailbox, your own user id on a Shortext endpoint.

## Conversation and session

A **conversation** is the thread between one contact and one of your channels. Inside it, a **session** is the current period of activity:

- **active**: a session is open, the agent (or a human) answers;
- **archived**: no session is open; a new message opens a new session.

## Handover

When a person from your team takes a conversation over, it is in **handover**: the AI agent stops answering this contact until the conversation is handed back. Replying from the Inbox (or through the API) takes the conversation over by default. See [Handover and assignment](/inbox/handover-and-assignment).

## Assignee and tickets

A session can be **assigned** to a member of your company, who then follows it in **Inbox > Mine**. A conversation can also produce **tickets** to track a request until it is solved. See [Tickets](/inbox/tickets).

## Identifiers in the API

The API never exposes internal database ids. Agents look like `AGI-XXXXX`, members like `USR-XXXXX`, companies like `CMP-XXXXX`, Shortext endpoints like `ste_...` and webhook events like `evt_...`. Treat every id as an opaque string.
